01 / PROFILE

Research with an adversarial mindset, evidence first.

I am Francisco Javier García Romero, operating publicly as CHIQUI_78. I participate in authorized vulnerability disclosure and bug bounty environments, with a focus on understanding how real systems fail across technical and business boundaries.

My work prioritises reproducibility, measurable impact and responsible disclosure. The goal is not simply to identify an endpoint or a misconfiguration, but to understand the assumption behind it and validate whether it can become a meaningful attack path.

04 / RESPONSIBLE DISCLOSURE

Evidence, coordination, then publication.

Security findings are handled through the applicable VDP, bug bounty or coordinated-disclosure process. Technical write-ups are published only when disclosure conditions allow it.

  • No publication of confidential program or customer data.
  • Reproduction steps are documented with the minimum data required to prove impact.
  • Vendor remediation and disclosure timelines are respected.
  • Public write-ups focus on lessons, root cause and defensive value.
